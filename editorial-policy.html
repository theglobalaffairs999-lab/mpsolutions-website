Editorial policy

How we report, source and correct what we publish. Last updated: 13 November 2026.

1. Purpose

MP Solutions publishes election trackers, constituency analysis, Andhra Pradesh current affairs, explainers on government schemes and policy, and study material for civil-services aspirants. This policy applies to everything published on mpsolutionsindia.com and on our social media channels.

2. Independence from consulting work

MP Solutions also advises candidates and parties. Published content is kept separate from that work: we do not publish client data, we do not slant analysis to help a client, and we disclose a relationship where it is relevant to a piece. Where we have a conflict of interest, we either disclose it or do not publish.

3. Sourcing and verification

  • Election figures come first from the Election Commission of India or the State Election Commission. Where official figures are not yet available, we use established news organisations (for example Eenadu, Sakshi, Andhra Jyothy, The Hindu, Deccan Chronicle) and label the figures as unofficial.
  • Every statistic, quotation and date is checked against a primary or reputable secondary source before publication. We do not invent statistics or present estimates as facts.
  • Government scheme and policy content is checked against the relevant Government Order, Gazette notification, Budget document or official portal.
  • Exam material is checked against the official notification and standard reference texts.

4. What we do not publish

  • Unverified allegations against any individual, party or official.
  • Fabricated results, exit-poll numbers or quotations.
  • Misleading or sensational headlines that the article does not support.
  • Content copied from other publishers. Short quotations are attributed and linked.
  • Hate speech, or content that targets people on the basis of religion, caste, gender or community.
  • Exit-poll or opinion-poll results during any period in which the Election Commission prohibits their publication.

5. Original content and the use of tools

Articles are written by our team. We use software, including AI tools, for research assistance, drafting support, translation between Telugu and English, charts and proofreading. Every published piece is reviewed, fact-checked and approved by a named member of the team, who is responsible for its accuracy.

6. Labelling

News reports, analysis and opinion are clearly distinguished. Trackers state whether the data is official or compiled from media reports and show when they were last updated. Sponsored content, if ever published, will be labelled as such; advertising served by Google AdSense is separate from editorial content and does not influence it.

7. Corrections

We correct errors promptly. Significant corrections are noted on the page with the date. To report an error, email theglobalaffairs999@gmail.com with the page link and the correct information and its source.

8. Responsibility

Editorial responsibility for this Site rests with the co-founders, Gopaluni Sai Mahanth and Chintapalli Prakash.
