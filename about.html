About MP Solutions

A political consulting, survey and election-analysis firm working from Hyderabad and Vijayawada.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Who we are

MP Solutions is a political consulting and campaign-management startup co-founded by Chintapalli Prakash and Gopaluni Sai Mahanth. We work with candidates and parties in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana on constituency surveys, booth management, data analytics, campaign strategy and digital media. Our motto, Janam to Janadesh (from the people to the people's verdict), describes the work: listening to voters booth by booth and turning what they say into a plan a campaign can act on.

What this website publishes

Alongside our consulting work, mpsolutionsindia.com is where we publish our own election trackers and analysis. At present this includes the AP Urban Local Body Elections 2026 results tracker, which compiles party-wise ward tallies and mayor or chairperson outcomes for every municipal corporation, municipality and nagar panchayat in Andhra Pradesh. We are expanding the site into a regular source of Andhra Pradesh current affairs, constituency and election analysis, explainers on government schemes, and study material for APPSC and UPSC aspirants. How we research and correct that content is set out in our editorial policy.

Where we have worked on the ground

  • Rajahmundry municipality – division-level survey and campaign work.
  • Kalluru mandal, Sattupalli assembly constituency (Telangana) – constituency survey and field operations.
  • Secondary-data studies of past elections in Kerala, Tamil Nadu, West Bengal and other states.

Our principles

  • Non-partisan publishing. Our consulting clients may belong to any party. The election data and analysis we publish on this site are presented without favour to any party or candidate.
  • Sources over speculation. Figures on this site are compiled from the State Election Commission, the Election Commission of India and established news organisations, and are labelled as official or unofficial.
  • Confidentiality for clients. We never publish client surveys, internal numbers or strategy.
  • Democratic values. We do not work on, or publish, content that incites hatred, spreads disinformation or targets voters on the basis of religion or caste.
