Privacy policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026. This policy explains what data this website collects, why, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

This website, mpsolutionsindia.com (the "Site"), is operated by MP Solutions, a political consulting and digital media firm with offices in Hyderabad and Vijayawada, India. Contact: theglobalaffairs999@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you email us, we receive your email address, name and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to reply and to keep a record of the correspondence.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the Site collects standard technical data: IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, pages visited, time spent, and approximate location derived from the IP address. This is collected through cookies and similar technologies by the services listed below.

3. Cookies and how they are used

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. The Site uses:

  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4). We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the Site (which pages are read, from where, on which devices). Google processes this data on our behalf; IP addresses are not logged or stored by Google Analytics 4. See How Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Advertising cookies (Google AdSense). We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners, as third-party vendors, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Functional storage. The election-results tracker loads data from a published Google Sheet and does not store any personal data on your device.

4. Your choices about advertising and analytics

  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You may opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.eu.
  • You may prevent Google Analytics from using your data with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the Site will still work, though some features may behave differently.

5. Third-party services and links

The Site embeds or links to services operated by others, including Google (Analytics, AdSense, Fonts, Sheets) and Instagram. Those services have their own privacy policies, which govern the data they collect. Advertisers appearing on the Site may use their own cookies and tracking technologies; we do not control them.

6. How we use the information

To operate and improve the Site, measure readership, display and measure advertising, respond to enquiries, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

7. Data retention and security

Email correspondence is kept for as long as needed to deal with the enquiry and any follow-up. Analytics data is retained for the period set in Google Analytics (currently 14 months) and then deleted or aggregated. The Site is served over HTTPS.

8. Children

The Site is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

9. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and other applicable laws, you may ask what personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw consent. Write to theglobalaffairs999@gmail.com; we respond within 30 days.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the Site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
