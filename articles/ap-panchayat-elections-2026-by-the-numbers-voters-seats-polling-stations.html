Andhra Pradesh is electing village sarpanches and ward members in three phases on 26 November, 29 November and 2 December 2026. This is a reference sheet of the key numbers released by the State Election Commission (SEC) and reported by the media, with simple calculations by our desk.

Polling 7 am to 1 pm; counting from 2 pm the same day. Seat counts for Phases 1 and 3 had not been published in the reports we reviewed. Some reports put the total number of panchayats at 13,171; SEC figures reported by Andhra Jyothy give 13,167 sarpanch seats.

Unanimous panchayats get a state incentive of ₹15 lakh (population under 5,000), ₹25 lakh (5,001–10,000) and ₹50 lakh (above 10,000), if the sarpanch and all ward members are elected unanimously.

Averages worth knowing

  • About 1,986 voters per gram panchayat on average.
  • About 10 wards per panchayat on average.
  • About 194 voters per polling station, so booths are small and counting is quick.

Compared with 2021

In the 2021 panchayat elections, polls were held for 13,097 panchayats in four phases, and the final phase recorded 82.85% turnout. The 2026 elections cover 13,167 sarpanch seats in three phases.

Quick answers

How many voters are there in the AP panchayat elections 2026?
2,61,52,377 voters: 1,28,76,887 men, 1,32,74,208 women and 1,282 others, according to State Election Commission figures reported by the media.

How many sarpanch and ward member seats are there?
13,167 sarpanch seats and 1,32,514 ward member seats. Of these, 6,445 sarpanch seats and 66,170 ward seats are reserved for women.

How many polling stations are there?
1,34,847 polling stations, of which 28,568 are classified as highly sensitive and 35,642 as sensitive.

What is the BC reservation in AP rural local bodies?
34%, restored by GO Ms 105 in August 2026 after it had been reduced to 24.15% earlier.

What is the spending limit for a sarpanch candidate?
₹2.5 lakh in panchayats with population under 10,000 and ₹4 lakh above that. Ward members: ₹60,000 and ₹1 lakh.

2021తో పోలిక

2021 పంచాయతీ ఎన్నికల్లో 13,097 పంచాయతీలకు నాలుగు దశల్లో పోలింగ్ జరిగింది. చివరి దశలో 82.85% పోలింగ్ నమోదైంది. 2026లో 13,167 సర్పంచ్ స్థానాలకు మూడు దశల్లో ఎన్నికలు.

బీసీ రిజర్వేషన్ ఎంత?
34%. ఆగస్టు 2026లో జీవో ఎంఎస్ 105 ద్వారా పునరుద్ధరించారు. గతంలో 24.15%కి తగ్గించారు.

సర్పంచ్ అభ్యర్థి ఖర్చు పరిమితి ఎంత?
10,000 లోపు జనాభా ఉన్న పంచాయతీల్లో ₹2.5 లక్షలు, ఆపైన ₹4 లక్షలు. వార్డు సభ్యులకు ₹60,000, ₹1 లక్ష.
