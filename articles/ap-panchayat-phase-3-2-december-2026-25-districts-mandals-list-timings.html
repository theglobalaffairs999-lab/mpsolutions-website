By MP Solutions Election Desk · Published 22 November 2026 · Explainer

Phase 3 of the Andhra Pradesh gram panchayat elections is on Wednesday, 2 December. According to the State Election Commission (SEC) notification, as reported by Deccan Chronicle, it covers 212 mandals in 25 districts. Find your mandal below, with polling hours, counting time and the key dates.

7 am – 1 pmPolling hours
2 pmCounting, same day

Key dates

StepDate
Notice and ward-wise voter lists21 November
Nominations21 to 23 November, 10.30 am to 5 pm
Appeals against rejectionUntil 5 pm, 25 November (disposed 26 November)
Withdrawal deadline and final candidate list27 November, 3 pm
Polling2 December, 7 am to 1 pm
Counting; deputy sarpanch electionFrom 2 pm, 2 December
Re-poll, if ordered3 December, 7 am to 1 pm; counting from 2 pm

Phase 3 is the last phase. Konaseema and Tirupati districts are not in this phase.

Quick answers

When are the Phase 3 results?

Counting starts at 2 pm on 2 December, right after polling closes at 1 pm. Follow our village search and the phase-wise results tracker.

Who elects the deputy sarpanch?

The newly elected ward members, on the same day.

త్వరిత సమాధానాలు

3వ విడత ఫలితాలు ఎప్పుడు?

పోలింగ్ మధ్యాహ్నం 1కి ముగిశాక, అదే రోజు 2 గంటల నుంచి లెక్కింపు. మా గ్రామ సెర్చ్, ఫలితాల పేజీ చూడండి.

ఉప సర్పంచ్‌ను ఎవరు ఎన్నుకుంటారు?

కొత్తగా ఎన్నికైన వార్డు సభ్యులు, అదే రోజు.
