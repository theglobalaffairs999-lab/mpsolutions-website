AP Municipal Elections 2026: TDP wins two-thirds of all wards

Party-wise results for all 100 urban local bodies, the 13 corporation mayors, and what the verdict shows.

Home › Articles › AP Municipal Elections 2026

By MP Solutions Election Desk · Published 13 November 2026 · Analysis

The ruling Telugu Desam Party (TDP) won 2,594 of 3,942 wards (65.8%) across Andhra Pradesh's 100 urban local bodies, according to the MP Solutions results tracker. Together with its NDA partners Jana Sena (487 wards) and the BJP (153 wards), the alliance holds 3,234 wards, or 82.0%. The opposition YSR Congress Party (YSRCP) won 561 wards (14.2%).

How the election was held

The State Election Commission (SEC) first scheduled polling for 28 October 2026, then moved it to 1 November after a Supreme Court direction, with re-polling on 2 November and counting from 3 November. The elections were conducted with ballot papers rather than EVMs. Of the 100 bodies, 92 went to the polls; in the remaining 8, every ward was decided unopposed, so no voting was needed there. The South First reported a turnout of 62.16%, with 60.22 lakh of 96.89 lakh registered voters casting ballots. Chimakurthi recorded the highest turnout (84.36%) and Tirupati the lowest (46.85%).

The 13 municipal corporations

The NDA won 12 of the 13 mayor posts: ten went to the TDP and two, Vijayawada and Eluru, to Jana Sena. The YSRCP's only mayor post is Anantapur, where it won 21 of 62 wards against the TDP's 30. In Greater Visakhapatnam, the largest corporation with 120 wards, the TDP won 64 and Jana Sena 26.

Who leads the municipalities and nagar panchayats

Of the 100 chairs of corporations, municipalities and nagar panchayats, the TDP holds 70, Jana Sena 20, the BJP 4, the YSRCP 4 and independents 2. Within the alliance, Jana Sena's chairs are spread across the Godavari and coastal districts, including Pithapuram (19 of 36 wards to Jana Sena), Tenali, Narasapuram, Amalapuram, Nidadavolu and Palakonda.

The BJP's strongest local showings were in Jammalamadugu (10 of 32 wards) and Yerraguntla (9 of 28), both in Kadapa district.

Where the YSRCP held on, and where it was wiped out

The YSRCP won the most wards in Nandyal (28 of 52), Nandikotkur (23 of 32) and Betamcherla (18 of 28), and held a strong minority in Anantapur, Kadiri and Vizianagaram. Its chairperson posts are Nandikotkur, Naidupeta and Betamcherla, plus the Anantapur mayor post.

At the other end, the party won no wards at all in 22 bodies, including Kuppam, Macherla, Proddatur, Tadipatri, Rayachoti and Piduguralla. The result in Pulivendula, the home constituency of YSRCP president Y.S. Jagan Mohan Reddy, where the TDP won 34 of 40 wards, drew wide attention.

What the parties said

TDP leaders described the verdict as a mandate for Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu's government; Minister Nara Lokesh called it a mandate to serve rather than to celebrate. YSRCP president Jagan Mohan Reddy alleged large-scale irregularities across the process, from nominations to counting. These are claims by the parties; MP Solutions has not independently verified them.

Why this matters

The municipal polls were one of the first large tests of the NDA government since the 2024 Assembly election. They show the TDP–Jana Sena–BJP alliance transferring votes in urban areas, Jana Sena establishing itself in the coastal and Godavari districts, and the YSRCP's urban base shrinking to pockets in the Nandyal and Anantapur regions. The rural results that followed pointed the same way: Telangana Today reported that the NDA won 499 of 582 ZPTC seats and 5,861 of 7,768 MPTC seats. Gram panchayat (sarpanch) elections are next.
