Markapuram constituency: every result, 1989–2024

Winners, margins and the three families who have shaped Assembly Constituency No. 111.

Home › Articles › Markapuram constituency

By MP Solutions Election Desk · Published 13 November 2026 · Constituency profile

Markapuram (Assembly Constituency No. 111) is held by Kandula Narayana Reddy of the TDP, who won in 2024 by 13,979 votes. It is one of the most closely fought seats in the western Prakasam region: no party has won it three times in a row since 1999, and three political families have traded it between them for 35 years.

Constituency at a glance

  • Lok Sabha seat: Ongole (one of its seven Assembly segments).
  • District: Markapuram district, formed on 31 December 2025 from the western part of Prakasam district, with Markapuram town as its headquarters.
  • Mandals: Markapuram, Tarlupadu, Konakanamitla and Podili.
  • Reservation: General (unreserved). Electors in 2019: about 2.1 lakh.

How close the contests have been

Margins have swung between 2,531 votes (1989) and over 20,000 (1994 and 2004). Since 2009 every margin has stayed between about 9,000 and 19,000 votes, so a shift of roughly 5,000–10,000 voters has been enough to change the result.

Three families, one seat

Markapuram politics has revolved around three names:

  • Kunduru family. Kunduru Pedda Konda Reddy won for the Congress in 1989, 1999 and 2004. Kunduru Nagarjuna Reddy won for the YSRCP in 2019 with a margin of 18,667, before moving to the neighbouring Giddalur seat in 2024.
  • Janke Venkata Reddy. Lost to Kunduru in 1989 and 1999, won as an Independent in 1994, and won again for the YSRCP in 2014.
  • Kandula Narayana Reddy. The TDP's candidate in every election since 2004. He has won twice (2009 and 2024) and lost three times. His 2024 win came with his highest vote share yet: 51.85%.

Not a bellwether

Unlike many AP seats, Markapuram often goes against the statewide wave. It elected an Independent in the 1994 TDP wave, stayed with the Congress in 1999 when the TDP won the state, chose the TDP in 2009 when the Congress returned, and chose the YSRCP in 2014 when the TDP formed the government. Since 2019 it has moved with the state.

The 2026 municipal result

In the November 2026 urban local body elections, the TDP won 31 of the 40 wards in Markapur municipality, with Jana Sena winning 6, the BJP 2 and the YSRCP 1. See the full figures in our AP municipal results tracker and our statewide analysis.

What to watch next

Markapuram became a district headquarters on 31 December 2025, a change every party will try to claim credit for. The gram panchayat elections in the four mandals are the next test of whether the TDP's 2024 and 2026 gains hold in the villages.
