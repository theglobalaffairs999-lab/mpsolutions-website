By MP Solutions Election Desk · Published 26 November 2026, 1:00 pm IST · Data

Polling in Phase 1 of the Andhra Pradesh gram panchayat elections reached 53.86% statewide by 11 am, up from 16.25% at 9 am, according to figures reported by Andhra Jyothy. The district-wise table below covers all 26 districts. Voting closed at 1 pm and counting starts at 2 pm, so the final turnout will be higher.

Kadapa's 20.22% is far below every other district, which may reflect delayed reporting rather than low voting; check the SEC's final figure. Other outlets reported slightly different local figures: The Hans India put NTR at 46.03% at 11 am against 43.11% in this table. Visakhapatnam and Guntur have no Phase 1 polling.

What stands out

  • Alluri Sitharama Raju (75.02%), Sri Sathya Sai (67.70%), Markapuram (64.02%), Anantapur (64.01%) and Kakinada (62.13%) led.
  • Polavaram (34.35%), Vizianagaram (43.73%), NTR (43.11%) and Srikakulam (44.01%) were lowest after Kadapa.
  • Turnout jumped about 37 points between 9 am and 11 am, the usual late-morning surge in village polls.

Municipal bodies

In the 23 urban local bodies voting the same day, turnout was 13.26% at 9 am, as reported by urgent.news. Polling there runs until 5 pm and counting is on 30 November. See our municipal polls report.

పట్టణ సంస్థలు

అదే రోజు 23 పట్టణ స్థానిక సంస్థల్లో ఉదయం 9 గంటలకు 13.26% పోలింగ్ (urgent.news). అక్కడ సాయంత్రం 5 వరకు పోలింగ్, 30 నవంబర్‌న లెక్కింపు.

తర్వాత ఏమిటి

  • గ్రామ పంచాయతీల లెక్కింపు మధ్యాహ్నం 2 గంటల నుంచి. మా లైవ్ ఫలితాల ట్రాకర్‌ చూడండి.
