Research and data services

Election data, voter research and constituency analysis for candidates, parties, media and researchers in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.

Request a proposal

The analysis published on this website comes from the same desk that works with campaigns. If you need that work done for your constituency, mandal or ward, or for a story or study, we prepare it to your brief.

What we offer

Constituency and mandal data reports

Past results, turnout trends, reservation history and voter composition for your area, compiled from official sources into one clear report.

Booth-wise analysis

Booth-level past results to identify strong, weak and swing booths and where turnout is being lost.

Voter surveys

Questionnaire design, sampling, field teams in Telugu and English, and a written report with findings you can act on.

Campaign strategy and booth management

A campaign plan built on the numbers, booth committees and polling-day coordination.

Digital campaign and content

Reels, creatives and daily social media content in Telugu, made to a professional standard.

Data for media and research

Election data tables, charts and explainers for news channels, YouTube creators, students and researchers.

Who we work with

Candidates contesting at every level from ward to Assembly, party units of any party, news organisations and creators, and researchers and students of elections.

How it works

  1. Tell us what you need. Use the enquiry form with your area, the election and the kind of work.
  2. Get a proposal. Within two working days we send the scope, the timeline and the cost.
  3. We do the work. You get regular updates while it is in progress.
  4. Report and walkthrough. We deliver the report or material and explain what it means for you.

Where we have worked on the ground

  • Rajahmundry municipality: division-level survey and campaign work.
  • Kalluru mandal, Sattupalli assembly constituency (Telangana): constituency survey and field operations.
  • Secondary-data studies of past elections in Kerala, Tamil Nadu, West Bengal and other states.

Confidentiality and honesty

Client work, surveys and strategy are never published or shared. Everything on the public part of this website comes from public sources. We give an honest reading of the data, and we do not promise any election result.
