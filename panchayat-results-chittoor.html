Method and sources

We compile village-wise sarpanch results from reports by established Telugu and English news organisations such as Sakshi, Eenadu, Andhra Jyothy, The Hindu and Hans India, and from public releases of the State Election Commission. Each entry names its source and the time it was added. When two sources disagree, we keep the first reported figure and note the conflict. After the SEC publishes its official data, we replace the media-reported entries with the confirmed ones and mark them SEC. Statewide and district numbers are on the phase-wise results tracker, with the calculation shown.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my village missing? Results are added only after a named news source reports them. Counting starts at 2 pm, and large mandals can take until evening. Check back later.

Are these results official? Entries marked Unofficial come from media reports. Entries marked SEC are confirmed by the State Election Commission.

Does the page show party names? No. Gram panchayat elections in Andhra Pradesh are held without party symbols, so we list the winning sarpanch only.

How do I report a mistake? Write to theglobalaffairs999@gmail.com with the village name and a link to a source.
