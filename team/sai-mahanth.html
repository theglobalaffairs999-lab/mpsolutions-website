Sai Mahanth

Co-founder and Political Strategist, MP Solutions

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Sai Mahanth (Gopaluni Sai Mahanth) is a co-founder and political strategist at MP Solutions, a political consulting, survey and election-analysis firm working from Vijayawada and Hyderabad. He is an Advocate of the Andhra Pradesh High Court and a broadcaster with All India Radio, Markapuram.

Role at MP Solutions

Sai Mahanth leads research, election analysis and the editorial side of the MP Solutions website. His qualifications are B.A., LL.B., MBA (Digital Marketing) and a PG Diploma in Journalism and Mass Communication.

Qualifications

B.A., LL.B., MBA (Digital Marketing), PG Diploma in Journalism and Mass Communication.

About MP Solutions

MP Solutions works with candidates and parties in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana on constituency surveys, booth management, data analytics, campaign strategy and digital media. Read more on the About page or send an enquiry.
